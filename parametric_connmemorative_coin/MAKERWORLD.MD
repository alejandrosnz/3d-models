Parametric Commemorative Coin & Coaster — Multicolor (AMS-ready)



Make commemorative coins and coasters with classic coin-style borders: curved text, banners, stars, beads and fine lines. The center gets a parametric motif (big number, laurel wreath, sunburst, star/compass rose, shield, or banknote-style guilloché rosette). Everything prints in up to 4 colors with the Bambu Lab AMS.



The design is flat, color only, with no relief — every color sits flush with the top face.



📖 Full parameter guide here

✨ Features

6 center motifs, each with its own text

Free color assignment in the center (motif, accents, text, background can use any of the 4 slots)

3 ready-made sizes + fully custom dimensions (Coaster Ø90×2 mm, Coin Ø50×3 mm, Coin Ø35×3 mm)

5 classic border layouts: top & bottom text, 360° text, top text + stars, banner + stars, banner + top text

Optional double-sided coin (mirrored back, different motif/text possible)

Chamfered edges, decorations (fillets, beads, separators, star arc)

AMS-ready: each color is a separate part — just assign filaments and print

🚀 Quick start

Click Customize.

In 1. Design, choose size and border layout.

In 2. Text, type your border texts.

In 3. Center motif, choose a motif and type the center text (e.g. 25 / YEARS).

Click Generate, then open the 3MF in Bambu Studio.

Assign one filament to each of the 4 parts, slice and print with the colored face down.

🖨️ Printing tips

Print with the colored face down (better results and fewer waste).

Use a 0.2 mm nozzle on the Ø35 coin.

❓ FAQ

Motif disappeared? It probably shares a slot with the center background — check slots in section 3.

Letters unevenly spaced? OpenSCAD uses Arial-like metrics; tweak Letter spacing.

Fewer than 4 colors? Assign the same filament to two parts in Bambu Studio.

Own logo? Set Center design = None (or use the border-only version), then add your SVG/STL as a part flush with the face.
