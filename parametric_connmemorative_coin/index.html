🪙 Coin & Coaster Guide

Commemorative Coin & Coaster

A parametric generator for commemorative coins and coasters printed in up to 4 colors with the Bambu Lab AMS. The design is flat, color only, with no relief: every color sits flush with the top face.

This guide explains every parameter in the customizer. Section numbers match what you see in MakerWorld.

Anatomy of the coin

The design is built in rings, from the outside in. The parameters change the size of each ring and what goes inside it.

Color slots

The model is exported as 4 parts, one per AMS slot. The colors you pick in the customizer are preview only. You choose the real filaments in Bambu Studio.

Quick start

  1. Click Customize on the MakerWorld model page.
  2. In Design, choose the size and the text layout.
  3. In Text, type your texts.
  4. In Center motif, choose a motif and type the center text, for example 25 / YEARS.
  5. Click Generate, then open the 3MF in Bambu Studio.
  6. Assign one filament to each of the 4 parts, then slice and print with the colored face up.

Printing tips

Colored face down

Print with the colored face down: the color is only on the first layers, so filament changes happen only there, purge waste is much lower than with full-height multicolor, and the colors print directly on the plate for a smoother finish.

Layer height

Use 0.2 mm, or 0.16 mm for small coins. Keep Color depth a multiple of the layer height. The default is 0.6 mm, which is 3 layers. For the Ø35 coin, use only a 0.2 mm nozzle for better detail.

Minimum detail

Lines of 0.8 mm (2 lines with a 0.4 mm nozzle) print reliably. Text on the Ø35 coin is about 1.7 mm tall, so use bold fonts and, for this size, a 0.2 mm nozzle.

Ironing

Ironing the top surface gives a smooth, shiny coin finish.

Double-sided

The first layer becomes the back face, so keep the bed clean. A smooth PEI plate gives a glossy back.

Delicate motifs

Guilloché and many-ray sunbursts are the most delicate motifs. On small coins, keep the counts low and check the slicer preview.

FAQ

Letters on the ring look unevenly spaced

OpenSCAD can't measure exact letter widths, so the arc spacing uses Arial-like metrics. With other fonts, adjust Letter spacing.

The top and bottom texts overlap

Shorten them, or lower Text size or Text fill. A warning also appears in the console.

My center motif disappeared

It is probably assigned to the same slot as the center background. Check the slot settings in Center motif.

Can I use fewer than 4 colors?

Yes. Assign the same filament to two parts in Bambu Studio.

Can I add my own logo?

Use the border-only version, or set the center motif to None. Then add your SVG or STL as a part in Bambu Studio, flush with the top face and as deep as Color depth.

A font doesn't change anything

If a font or style isn't available, OpenSCAD silently falls back to a default font. Try another family or style.
